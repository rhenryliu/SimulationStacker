# FRB DM–galaxy cross-correlation in the simulations: predictions

**Written 2026-10-20, before any run of `scripts/cross_corr/make_frb_dm_xi.py`.** Dated predictions file required by `decisions.md` D-20. The results go into `records/frb_dm_xi_record.md`, scored against this file. Thresholds are fixed here and are not to be moved after the runs.

## The task

Simulate Figure 5 of McCarty et al. (arXiv:2608.11296): the FRB dispersion-measure–galaxy cross-correlation $\xi_{g,\delta{\rm DM}}(R)$, in pc cm$^{-3}$, against transverse comoving separation, 15 log bins over 0.3–30 cMpc. The paper builds it from FLAMINGO lightcone DM shells with each shell's mean subtracted, random pixels as FRBs, and lightcone subhalos matched to the BGS $N(z)$; its "NFW" curve is the dark-matter overdensity times the mean DM.

Here, on single snapshots with the programme's map-level estimator (D-01):

$$
\xi_{g,\delta{\rm DM}}(R) \simeq (1+z)\,\bar N_e\,\langle\delta_e\rangle_{[a,b)},\qquad
\xi^{\rm NFW}(R) \simeq (1+z)\,\bar N_e\,\langle\delta_m\rangle_{[a,b)},
$$

where $\langle\delta_X\rangle_{[a,b)}$ is the annulus mean of the projected overdensity over the log bin $[a,b)$, averaged over the galaxies, and $\bar N_e$ is the mean comoving free-electron column of the projected `ionized_gas` map. The box depth cancels between $\bar N_e$ and $\delta_e$. The $(1+z)$ is the constant-redshift approximation of the paper's own Appendix A. The NFW curve uses $m = t - b$ (D-02) and the same $\bar N_e$, as the paper multiplies the dark-matter overdensity by the mean DM.

User decisions (2026-10-20): the BGS SHAM sample of this repo (stellar-mass abundance matching at $10^{-3}\,(h^{-1}{\rm cMpc})^{-3}$, parent cap $5\times10^{14}\,M_\odot/h$, satellites included) and no stellar-mass sub-cuts; all four runs (FLAMINGO L1_m9, fgas$-8\sigma$, Jet_fgas$-4\sigma$ at $z=0.30$, snapshot 71; TNG300-1 at its existing snapshot 80, $z=0.261$); `yz` projection; no data overlay.

## Already seen, so not predictions

- In TNG300-1 at snapshot 80 this SHAM sample has 8,615 galaxies with $M_* \ge 9.0\times10^{10}\,M_\odot$ (median $1.4\times10^{11}$), so a $M_*>10^{10}$ cut would not change it and $M_*>10^{11}$ keeps 84 per cent. Measured 2026-10-20 while planning.
- The ordering of the FLAMINGO variants in related quantities: in the unbound-gas Figure 1 variant of 2026-10-20 (ionized gas over total, $\Delta\Sigma$ and $\Sigma$ ring filters, mass-cut haloes, $z=0.5$), fgas$-8\sigma$ is lowest at small radii, Jet next, fiducial highest, and TNG300-1 above FLAMINGO fiducial.

## Predictions

**P1. Large scales: electrons trace matter.** For every run, $\xi_e/\xi^{\rm NFW}$ of the same run lies within $\pm0.10$ of unity in every bin above 10 cMpc.
*My prior: 0.93–1.00 in FLAMINGO, slightly below 1 from gas expelled beyond the halo.*

**P2. Small scales: feedback suppression.** In the innermost bin (0.30–0.41 cMpc), $\xi_e/\xi^{\rm NFW} < 0.85$ in every hydro run.
*My prior: 0.4–0.7.*

**P3. Feedback ordering.** Over 0.3–3 cMpc, $\xi_e$ is ordered fiducial $>$ Jet_fgas$-4\sigma$ $>$ fgas$-8\sigma$ in at least 8 of the 10 bins.
*My prior: holds in all 10, by analogy with the related quantities above.*

**P4. The suppression is local.** The fgas$-8\sigma$ to fiducial ratio $\xi_e^{\rm fgas}/\xi_e^{\rm fid}$ is further from 1 at 0.3–1 cMpc than at 10–30 cMpc.
*My prior: about 0.6–0.8 inside 1 cMpc, 0.95–1.0 beyond 10 cMpc.*

**P5. Cross-code.** TNG300-1's $\xi_e/\xi^{\rm NFW}$ is above FLAMINGO fiducial's at $R < 1$ cMpc.
*My prior: yes. Caveat for the large-$R$ amplitudes: $\xi$ is an annulus mean, uncompensated, so its amplitude depends on the box scale (D-04); TNG300-1's 205 cMpc/$h$ box lacks the long modes of FLAMINGO's 1 cGpc, which can lower its large-$R$ $\xi$ by of order 10 per cent independently of the physics. The ratio to its own NFW curve largely cancels this.*

## What would not be comparable with the paper, whatever the outcome

Single snapshots instead of lightcones; $z = 0.30$ and $0.26$ against the paper's mean galaxy redshifts 0.17–0.22; no FRB-redshift weighting of the pairs; a SHAM sample instead of subhalos matched to the BGS $N(z)$ and tuned to lensing halo masses. The comparison is between simulations, not with the data.
