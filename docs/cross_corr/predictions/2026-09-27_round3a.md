# Round 3A predictions: window smearing or mediation failure?

**Written 2026-09-27, before any Round 3A run.** This is the dated predictions file that `decisions.md` D-20 requires. Each item states the test, what each reading predicts, the pass/fail threshold, and my own prior. Thresholds are fixed here and are not to be moved after the runs; the results go into `records/round3a_record.md`, scored against this file.

Notation follows `mathematical_formalism.md`. $C_{\mathcal F}(R)$ is the filtered calibration factor already in `data/cross_corr_C/`, $C(k) = P_{bm}P_{gm}/(P_{mm}P_{gb})$ its harmonic-space counterpart, $\eta = P_{bm}/P_{mm}$, $\beta = P_{gm}/P_{mm}$. The split measured in Stage 2 is

$$
C_{\mathcal F} = W_{\mathcal F}\,M_{\mathcal F},\qquad
M_{\mathcal F} = \frac{Y^{\rm med}_{gb}}{Y_{gb}},\quad Y^{\rm med}_{gb} = \int d\mu_R\,\frac{P_{bm}P_{gm}}{P_{mm}},\qquad
W_{\mathcal F} = \frac{\langle\eta\rangle\langle\beta\rangle}{\langle\eta\beta\rangle}\ \text{(Eq. 48)} .
$$

$M_{\mathcal F}$ is the part of $C_{\mathcal F}$ that exists only if mediation fails ($M_{\mathcal F}\equiv1$ when $C(k)\equiv1$); $W_{\mathcal F}$ is the window term that exists even under exact mediation.

## Predictions

**P1. O-01, the split at $1'$ ($\Delta\Sigma$, baryons, $z\approx0.5$).** Let $f_W = \ln W_{\mathcal F}/\ln C_{\mathcal F}$ at $R = 1'$.
- Window reading: $f_W \ge 0.8$ in all four runs, and $|C(k)-1| \le 0.02$ over $\Delta\Sigma(1')$'s response range ($k_{05}$–$k_{95}$).
- Mediation-failure reading: $f_W \le 0.2$ in all four runs, and $C(k) - 1 \ge 0.05$ somewhere in that range, ordered fiducial $<$ Jet $<$ fgas$-8\sigma$.
- Anything else is **mixed**: both terms are real and are booked separately.
- *My prior: mixed, with $M_{\mathcal F}(1') - 1$ between $+0.03$ and $+0.08$, ordered by feedback strength.* Reason: the SHAM hosts ($\sim10^{13}\,M_\odot/h$) are more gas-depleted than the $P_{mm}$-weighted halo population at $k\sim5\,h/$Mpc, so the residual $\epsilon$ of Eq. (46) correlates with the galaxies through halo mass alone, without invoking the galaxies' own AGN.

**P2. O-05 in harmonic space (the split of the fiducial sample by parent-halo mass at its median).** Mediation requires $C(k)$ to be the same for every galaxy sample.
- O-05's reading (direct galaxy–gas coupling): the high-mass half deviates most.
- *My prior (halo-mass-dependent depletion, as in P1): the low-mass half deviates most*, $C_{\rm lo}(k) - C_{\rm hi}(k) \ge 0.02$ at $k = 3$–$7\,h/$Mpc in the FLAMINGO fiducial run.
- Either way, if $|C_{\rm lo} - C_{\rm hi}| \le 0.01$ over that range in all four runs, sample invariance holds at the per-cent level.

**P3. O-06, what the $z\approx0.26$ cross-code gap follows.** The committed gap ($\Delta\Sigma$, baryons) sits at $1'$.

- Snapshot/field reading: the gap stays with the snapshot.
- *My prior: it stays with the snapshot and with the baryon field*: cross-code $|C_{\rm TNG}-C_{\rm fid}|/C$ at $1'$ stays $\ge 0.05$ at $z\approx0.26/0.30$ with the LRG-like density and $\le 0.02$ at $z\approx0.5$ with the BGS-like one. The electron $C$ stays within 0.04 across codes in all four combinations.

**P6. O-09, the $\Delta\Sigma$ log-slope.** Fit $d\ln Y^{(\Delta\Sigma)}_{gm}/d\ln R$ over $1'$–$9.75'$.

- A slope of about $-1.5$ means keeping it with a caveat.

**P7. O-02, the DoG sweep.** $\hat W_{\rm DoG} > 0$, so $W^{\rm DoG}_{\mathcal F}$ is a genuine covariance: with $\eta$ falling and $\beta$ rising it must be $>1$ at every scale and cannot change sign between runs.

- So at matched $k_{50}$, $|C^{\rm DoG}_{\mathcal F} - 1|$ is within a factor 1.5 of $|C^{(\Delta\Sigma)}_{\mathcal F} - 1|$, and the feedback ordering persists.
- The formalism's decision rule (§4.5) is kept as stated: "substantial shrinkage" means at least a factor 2 at matched $k_{50}$ in all four runs.
